Welcome to the Animal Kingdom! ⭐️

Get ready for a fun adventure! Today, we will learn amazing facts about different groups of animals.

1. Fantastic Furry Mammals

  • They are warm-blooded vertebrates (they have a backbone).
  • They have hair or fur to keep their bodies warm.
  • Mothers produce milk to feed their babies.
  • Most give birth to live young instead of laying eggs!

2. Incredible High-Flying Birds

  • They are warm-blooded animals covered in feathers.
  • They have lightweight, hollow bones to help them fly easily.
  • They lay hard-shelled eggs, usually in a nest.
  • They use special beaks to eat their food instead of teeth.

3. Spectacular Six-Legged Insects

  • They are invertebrates with a hard outer shell called an exoskeleton.
  • Their bodies have exactly 3 parts: head, thorax, and abdomen.
  • Every insect has 6 jointed legs.
  • Many go through amazing life changes called metamorphosis.
