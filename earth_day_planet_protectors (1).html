The Universal Date

April 22nd Every Year

Why April 22? In 1970, organizers chose this date because it sat between university Spring Break and final exams—ensuring millions of students could walk out into streets to champion clean air!

💡 Over 193 nations now celebrate Earth Day simultaneously!
📜
History in 1970

The Spark of Change

US Senator Gaylord Nelson and student coordinator Denis Hayes galvanized 20 million Americans. This led directly to creating the EPA, the Clean Air Act, and the Endangered Species Act!

🦅 Saved the Bald Eagle and Peregrine Falcon from extinction!

Continent Feature 8,000 km Living Wall

Africa: The Great Green Wall

Across 11 countries south of the Sahara Desert, millions of community members are planting an 8,000-kilometer wall of drought-resistant trees. This miraculous project combats desertification, restores fertile farmland, and cools regional temperatures!

🌱 Student Connection: Kids in Kenya and Senegal plant school baobab and acacia seedlings each April 22nd.
🚲

Car-Free Boulevards

Cities like Paris, Tokyo, and Bogotá close major downtown roads to cars so hundreds of thousands of people cycle, skate, and walk in clean air!

🪸

Coral Reef Nurseries

Volunteer divers in Fiji, Australia, and the Caribbean attach healthy coral fragments to underwater frames to regrow colorful marine nurseries.

🐝

Bee & Bug Hotels

Schools across Scandinavia and Germany build wooden shelters stuffed with bamboo rods and pinecones for solitary pollinator bees!

🎨

Recycled Sculpture Fests

Artists in Rio de Janeiro and London build giant life-sized whales and hawks entirely from recovered ocean plastics to raise awareness.

School & Home Actionable Superpower

🎒 Eco Action Lab: Student Power!

Students possess massive leverage to inspire families, classrooms, and neighborhoods. Try these daily missions!

🌿
🍱

Zero-Waste Bento Challenge

Swap cling plastic wraps and single-serving chip bags for washable bento boxes, silicone pouches, and cloth napkins. A single school can save 18,000 plastic wrappers each semester!

🪟

Windowsill Seedling Nursery

Save egg cartons and rinsed yogurt cups. Fill with potting soil and plant tomato or basil seeds. Place on sunny classroom windowsills and watch roots sprout through transparent cups!

🎨

Hallway Eco Awareness Posters

Design catchy cartoon posters above water fountains: "Drink Up, Don't Let It Run!" and by classroom light switches: "Kill the Watts When Leaving the Spot!"

🧤

10-Minute Recess Litter Sweepers

Form a squad of 3 friends with gloves. Sweep the soccer pitch perimeter and playground mulch before wind and rain carry candy wrappers down street storm drains into oceans!
